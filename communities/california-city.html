Desert Space and Unbeatable Value in California City

California City is California’s third-largest city by land area, offering expansive desert vistas, flat building sites, and some of the most affordable parcel pricing in all of Southern California. Located just 20 minutes north of Edwards Air Force Base, it is a prime choice for aerospace contractors, active-duty military personnel, and buyers seeking spacious acreage with room for workshops and RV storage.

Aspen II Homes specializes in desert custom building that maximizes value and longevity:

  • Flat Pad Grading & Simplified Foundations: The level topography of California City reduces excavation and grading costs significantly compared to mountainous terrain, allowing more of your investment to go into interior luxury finishes.
  • High-Efficiency Desert Thermal Envelopes: High summer temperatures require advanced climate engineering: 16 SEER high-efficiency cooling, radiant barrier roof sheathing, and Low-E solar-reflectance windows.
  • Heroes of the Nation Military Program: Dedicated build pricing incentives, flexible design allowances, and VA construction loan assistance for service members and veterans stationed at Edwards AFB.
