Precision Construction in the Indian Wells Valley

Located in northeastern Kern County surrounded by the dramatic southern Sierra Nevada, Ridgecrest serves as the civilian and military anchor for the premier Naval Air Weapons Station (NAWS) China Lake research facility. The high-desert desert environment demands a builder with rigorous structural standards and superior thermal knowledge.

Aspen II Homes constructs custom homes engineered specifically for Indian Wells Valley climate and geology:

  • Seismic Design Category D Structural Engineering: Rigorous hold-downs, anchor bolting, and shear-wall engineering designed to withstand desert seismic requirements.
  • Extreme Thermal Performance: Continuous exterior foam insulation barriers, 16 SEER high-efficiency split AC systems, and solar-reflective roof sheathing to combat summer heat and keep power bills minimal.
  • Heroes of the Nation Military Pricing: Dedicated housing packages, VA construction loan support, and closing fee credits for active-duty naval personnel, defense contractors, and veterans.
