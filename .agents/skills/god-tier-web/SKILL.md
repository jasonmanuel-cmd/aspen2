# God-Tier Web Design, Responsiveness & 3-Engine Domination Protocol

This skill provides the comprehensive architectural blueprint to build, rebuild, or optimize any commercial or professional website to the standard of the world's best web designers and technical SEO/AI engineers.

---

## 1. Visual Hierarchy & Luxury Architectural Aesthetic
- **Typographic Duality**: Pair an authoritative editorial serif (e.g. `Newsreader`, `Fraunces`, `Cormorant`) for headlines and pull-quotes with a clean geometric sans (e.g. `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`, `Geist`) for UI and data.
- **Fluid Typography**: Never use static font sizes. Enforce `font-size: clamp(min, preferred, max)` for all headings (`h1` through `h3`) and measure line lengths strictly between 55–75 characters.
- **Organic High-Value Color Palette**: Ground the brand in 5 intentional CSS custom properties:
  - `--ink`: Deep foundational background/contrast color (e.g. `#0E1712`).
  - `--bone`: Warm alabaster/linen background (e.g. `#FAF7F2`).
  - `--clay` / `--accent`: Warm terracotta, copper, or brand primary (e.g. `#B35B37`).
  - `--sage` / `--secondary`: Muted organic contrast (e.g. `#6E8263`).
  - `--sand`: Delicate warm border and card fill (e.g. `#EAE3D5`).
- **Hairline Aesthetics**: Use subtle borders (`rgba(0,0,0,0.08)` to `0.12`), frosted glass navigation with `backdrop-filter: blur(18px)`, and soft layered shadows rather than heavy drop shadows.

---

## 2. Flawless Multi-Device Responsiveness (Mobile, Tablet, Desktop)
- **Mobile Sticky Conversion Dock (< 768px)**:
  - Fixed floating dock at the screen bottom with 1-tap direct call/action and 1-tap consultation modal trigger.
  - Ensures seamless lead generation without forcing users to scroll back up.
- **Touch Ergonomics**:
  - Enforce minimum 48px × 48px clickable touch targets on all mobile buttons and interactive elements.
- **Mobile Slide-Over Drawer**:
  - Full-height drawer with frosted blur backdrop, animated hamburger icon (transforms to an X), and direct phone/email cards.
- **Tablet Grid Balance (768px – 1024px)**:
  - All 3-column and 4-column grids (bento, features, process, footer) must fold gracefully into 2-column balanced layouts, never awkward 1-column stretches or cramped 4-column squeezes.
- **Desktop Ultra-Wide (> 1024px)**:
  - Restrain container max-width (`1240px`–`1320px`) with centered auto margins, 4-column trust bars, and 3-column product/service cards.

---

## 3. Reputational Front-Loading & The Hard Proof Engine
- **The Top Credibility Bar**:
  - Front-load proof above the fold: Years in business, license numbers, ratings (BBB A+, Google 5-Star, BuildZoom Top 2%), permitted volume, or verified client count.
- **Specifics Over Superlatives**:
  - Never allow empty filler phrases like "unparalleled quality" or "meticulous attention to detail" without attaching hard numbers, dates, or named sources.
- **The Proactive Guarantee**:
  - Explicit written warranty or satisfaction guarantee (e.g. 1-2-10 warranty + scheduled return walkthrough) with a direct customer service dispatch link to eliminate buyer anxiety.

---

## 4. Interactive Lead-Capture Engines
- **Interactive Cost & Payment Estimator**:
  - Allow users to select location/community, plan/service tier, toggle add-on options, and immediately view calculated price estimates and monthly payment projections.
  - Provide a "Lock In Estimate" button that pre-populates the consultation inquiry modal.
- **Quick-Spec Product / Service Modals**:
  - Clicking any card opens an architectural or technical spec drawer with dimensions, inclusions, and a direct inquiry action.
- **Status Filtering Tabs**:
  - Dynamic buttons allowing instant filtering of inventory, portfolio, or services (e.g. *All*, *Ready Now*, *Under Construction*, *Coming Soon*).

---

## 5. Total 3-Engine Domination: SEO · AEO · GEO
- **SEO (Search Engine Optimization)**:
  - Single semantic `<h1>`, logical `<h2>`/`<h3>` hierarchy.
  - Hyperlocal geographic keyword targeting in title, description, and copy.
  - Complete Open Graph (`og:*`), Twitter Cards, and geo meta tags (`geo.region`, `geo.placename`, `geo.position`, `ICBM`).
  - Production-ready `sitemap.xml` including image namespaces and priorities.
- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)**:
  - Minimum 8–10 FAQs where headings are framed in the user's exact query format ("How much does X cost?").
  - First paragraph under each heading is a concise, direct 40–60 word answer containing the specific number, timeline, or fact.
- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)**:
  - Fact-dense, machine-extractable sentences engineered for LLMs (Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude).
  - Production-ready `llms.txt` at the domain root summarizing canonical business facts.
  - AI-crawler-friendly `robots.txt` explicitly allowing `GPTBot`, `OAI-SearchBot`, `Google-Extended`, `ClaudeBot`, `PerplexityBot`.
  - Comprehensive Schema.org JSON-LD `@graph` linking `Organization`, `LocalBusiness`, `hasCredential`, `hasOfferCatalog`, `Product`, `Service`, `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`, and `WebSite`.

---

## 6. Core Web Vitals & Zero-Bloat Performance
- Keep total page weight under 1.5MB.
- Use native modern HTML/CSS/vanilla JS — zero heavy client frameworks unless explicitly needed.
- Explicit `width` and `height` attributes on all images to eliminate Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS < 0.02).
- Eager load and `fetchpriority="high"` on hero images; lazy load all below-the-fold assets.
