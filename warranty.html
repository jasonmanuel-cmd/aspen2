Our Warranty Is In Writing

Comprehensive 1-2-10 Builder Warranty

We have built in Kern County since 1994. Homes get lived in, mountain seasons change, and what matters is having a local builder who answers the phone and stands behind their craft. Every Aspen II home closes with our full written 1-2-10 warranty.

Scheduled Follow-Through

The 11-Month Builder Walkthrough

High-elevation mountain climates cause wood framing to undergo natural seasonal expansion and contraction during its first four seasons. Rather than waiting for you to call, Aspen II Homes schedules a formal return walkthrough at month 11 of your occupancy to address natural settling, drywall touch-ups, and door re-alignments.

Month 11
Proactive Return Visit by Builder
We inspect door thresholds, caulk joints, drywall seams, and exterior weatherstripping before your 1-year mark ends.
