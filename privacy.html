Privacy Policy

How Aspen II Homes collects, uses, and protects the information you share with us on this website.

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Aspen II Homes, a homebuilder presenting new homes at Sunset Retreat in Tehachapi, California (“Aspen II Homes,” “we,” “us”). This policy covers the website at aspen2bakersfield.store. Questions about it can go to Aspen2homes@gmail.com or (661) 238-3136.

Information you give us

When you use the tour request form or the quick register form on our Contact page, you may give us:

  • Your name
  • Your email address and/or phone number
  • How you prefer to be contacted and when works for you
  • The home model or topic you're interested in

We also record the date and time you sent the form and which form you used. If you call, text, or email us, we receive whatever you include in that message, along with your phone number or email address.

Information collected automatically

  • Site analytics. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and understand which pages are useful. It records information such as the page visited, the referring site, and general device, browser, and country details. It does not use cookies to identify you.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider, Vercel, automatically processes technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the time of your visit in order to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Storage in your browser. The site stores a few items in your browser's session storage, which is cleared when you close the tab: whether you've already watched the opening video, and, after you submit a form, the details you entered so the confirmation page can show them back to you. These items stay on your device; we don't read them from our servers.

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use third-party advertising networks on this site.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and contact you by your preferred method
  • To schedule tours and send the plans, pricing, availability, and incentive details you asked for
  • To keep a record of our conversations about your new home
  • To maintain, secure, and improve this website

Who receives your information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run this website and respond to you:

  • Our customer relationship management (CRM) system, which receives form submissions so our team can follow up.
  • Formspree, a form-processing service that delivers form submissions to us by email as a backup.
  • Vercel, which hosts this website and provides the analytics described above.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

How long we keep it

We keep your contact details for as long as we're helping you with a home, and afterwards as long as reasonably needed for our records, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop contact. Tell us at any time and we'll stop reaching out.
  • Access, correction, and deletion. Ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We'll respond within 45 days.
  • California residents. Depending on the circumstances, California law may give you rights to know, correct, and delete personal information and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We honor access, correction, and deletion requests from any visitor, and we don't sell or share personal information, so there's nothing to opt out of. We will not discriminate against you for making a request.
  • Do Not Track. We don't track visitors across other websites, so we treat all visitors the same whether or not a browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

To make a request, email Aspen2homes@gmail.com or call (661) 238-3136. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on it.

Security

This site is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection, and we limit access to your information to the people and services that need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

This website is intended for adults shopping for a home. It isn't directed to children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Other websites

Links to other sites, such as lenders or map services, are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the effective date above.
