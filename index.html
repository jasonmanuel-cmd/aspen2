Your Questions

Planning your new home

Where does Aspen II Homes build?

Aspen II Homes is presenting new-construction homes at Sunset Retreat in Tehachapi, California. Contact the building team to confirm current lot availability, construction status, and whether your preferred home model is available for your intended location.

Which home models can I explore?

The collection includes Tranquil Oasis, Sunset Retreat, The Tranquil Abode, Sunrise View Residence, Enchanted Haven, and The Grand Haven. The Models page groups the supplied photography, blueprint PDF, and available specifications under each home’s name.

How much do the new homes cost?

The advertised starting price for the Sunset Retreat first release is $459,000. Current pricing depends on the home, lot, design, finishes, and available options. Ask the team for a current quote before relying on an advertised starting price.

Can I customize my new home?

Aspen II offers reserve-and-customize, semi-custom model selection, and fully custom building paths. Available choices depend on your model and construction stage. Ask the team which finishes, fixtures, cabinetry, and design changes can be selected before you reserve.

Where can I see the floor plan blueprints?

Visit the Models page to view the blueprint for each of the six homes. Each model section also links to the original PDF, which you can open for closer inspection. Confirm plan dimensions and current specifications with the team.

Is 0% down financing available?

The site advertises a 0% down option for eligible first-time buyers. Eligibility, lender approval, and program terms apply. Contact the team to discuss your situation and confirm available financing options; an advertised program is not a loan approval or commitment to lend.

How do early-buyer savings work?

The site advertises up to $15,000 in early-buyer savings toward your new home. Availability, eligibility, and program terms apply. Ask which credits are currently offered, how they can be used, and whether they apply to your selected home and reservation.

How do I request a tour or model information?

Use the Contact page to share your preferred model and contact details, or call (661) 238-3136. The team can discuss tours, current availability, customization, pricing, and construction timelines. Model inquiry links select the requested house in the contact form.
