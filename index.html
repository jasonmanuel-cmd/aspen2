The Home

Designer finishes meet everyday livability

Welcome to a fully reimagined Newbury Park residence framed by sweeping views of Boney Mountain. Luxury vinyl plank flooring flows throughout — the stage for a home remodeled top to bottom with genuine attention to detail.

The showpiece kitchen features dramatic gold-and-black marbled quartz countertops, a professional KitchenAid gas range and oven, and a chef's pot filler — equally suited to weeknight dinners and holiday gatherings. The living room fireplace, wrapped in imported Italian veneer, anchors the main space, and the same craftsmanship carries into both remodeled bathrooms with fully tiled showers.

Outside, the covered rear patio — finished in custom hexagon Italian tile — overlooks a beautifully landscaped yard. And then the surprise: a versatile bonus room off the garage spanning over 400 square feet — a home office, gym, studio, or media retreat.

All of it sits minutes from the area's International Baccalaureate middle and high school programs, plus trails, parks, shopping, and easy 101 access. Turnkey, timeless, truly move-in ready.
