Questions & Answers

Straight answers on building in Kern County.

Direct, extractable facts. Need custom guidance? Call our local team at (661) 238-3136.

How much does it cost to build a home with Aspen II Homes?
Aspen II Homes builds semi-custom homes ranging from roughly $449,000 to $590,000 depending on the chosen floor plan, lot topography, and interior finish selections. Our plans range from 1,507 to 2,444 square feet. Lot acquisition cost is separate if you do not already own land, and we conduct a comprehensive feasibility review before you commit.
How long does the build take from start to move-in?
Most custom and semi-custom builds take 7 to 10 months from signed contract to key turnover. The primary variables affecting build time are Kern County permit processing schedules and seasonal mountain weather during excavation and concrete pours. Homes already under construction in framing can close much sooner.
Can you build on a lot I already own?
Yes. We build on customer-owned lots throughout Kern County. We begin with a site walk and feasibility evaluation covering building setbacks, slope, soil stability, drainage, utility access (septic, well, or municipal), and HOA guidelines, delivering a transparent, firm price before construction contracts are signed.
What communities and geographic areas do you serve?
Aspen II Homes actively builds across Tehachapi, California City, Ridgecrest, Golden Hills, Bear Valley Springs, and Stallion Springs in Kern County, California. We coordinate directly with local building inspectors, utility districts, and gated community architectural committees across all six regions.
Are you licensed, bonded, and insured in California?
Yes. Aspen II Homes, Inc. holds an active California Contractors State License Board license #1004058 (Class B, General Building Contractor), valid through May 31, 2027. We hold a surety bond through Hudson Insurance Company and workers' compensation coverage through State Compensation Insurance Fund, and maintain an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau.
What warranty coverage comes with an Aspen II home?
Every home comes with a comprehensive written warranty: one year on workmanship and interior materials, two years on electrical, plumbing, and HVAC systems, and ten years on structural components including foundations and framing. We also include a scheduled 11-month warranty walkthrough to resolve any settlement items prior to the one-year mark.
Do you offer dedicated programs for military families and veterans?
Yes. Our Heroes of the Nation program provides dedicated pricing incentives, flexible allowances, and VA construction loan guidance for active-duty personnel and veterans stationed at Edwards Air Force Base and NAWS China Lake.
How much can I customize an Aspen II floor plan?
Because our homes are semi-custom, you can personalize cabinetry, quartz countertops, flooring, plumbing and lighting fixtures, exterior paint, patio extensions, and select non-structural interior walls. Structural modifications are engineered and priced upfront so you know exact numbers before finalizing.
