Our Heritage

Built by Tehachapi Locals, for Tehachapi Families.

Over the last three decades, Aspen II Homes has shaped the residential landscape of the Tehachapi Mountains. Founded and directed by general contractor Mark Sheehan, our company has navigated every shift in California Title 24 energy codes, Kern County wildfire building mandates (Chapter 7A), and high-elevation seismic standards.

Unlike national tract builders who subcontract to the lowest out-of-town bid and disappear when the keys change hands, Aspen II Homes is rooted right here. Our trade partners, framers, plumbers, and concrete specialists have worked with us for decades. We live in the community we build in, and our reputation is on display in every neighborhood from Bear Valley Springs to Golden Hills.

The Aspen Difference

What Guides Every Hammer Swing

We don't build tract houses. We build enduring mountain homes that families pass down through generations.

1. Upfront Transparency

No bait-and-switch pricing or surprise change orders. When we quote a base build price of $449,900, it includes engineering, permits, quartz counters, and owned solar.

2. High-Altitude Engineering

Mountain living demands better construction: heavy-gauge seismic shear framing, R-21 wall and R-38 ceiling insulation, and frost-depth post-tension slabs.

3. Written Accountability

A builder's promise is only as good as what's written down. We provide an enforceable 1-2-10 warranty backed by our scheduled 11-month return walkthrough.
