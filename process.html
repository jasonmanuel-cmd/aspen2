Predictable, Disciplined Construction

Eight Steps from Feasibility to Keys

Building a custom home should be transparent, organized, and exciting. With 31 years of Kern County building discipline, Aspen II Homes coordinates every soil test, structural calculation, and county inspection on a predictable 7 to 10 month timeline.

01 Week 1–2

Lot Discovery & Feasibility Analysis

Before any contracts or design commitments, our general contractor visits your parcel in person. We evaluate topography, slope, setbacks, fire defensible space, soil type, and utility tie-in availability to ensure there are no surprises down the road.

  • Topographical slope and driveway grading evaluation
  • Setback verification against Kern County or HOA zoning
  • CSD water meter or private well feasibility review
  • Engineered septic vs. municipal sewer determination
02 Week 3–4

Semi-Custom Plan & Material Selections

Choose from our six proven mountain floor plans (1,507 to 2,444 SF) or our military Heroes plan (1,705 SF). Personalize your exterior elevation, covered mountain veranda, 3-car garage layout, quartz slab counters, cabinetry stains, and flooring.

  • Architectural layout customization & veranda expansions
  • Interior finish selections: quartz, soft-close cabinets, vinyl plank
  • HVAC and owned rooftop solar system sizing
  • Guaranteed base pricing lock with transparent upgrade menu
03 Month 2–3

Engineering, Title 24 & Permitting

Our structural engineers draft the full architectural blueprints, geotechnical foundation design, and Title 24 energy calculations required for high-elevation Kern County mountain building. We submit and manage the plan check directly with the building department.

  • Site plot plan & civil grading drawings
  • Post-tension foundation engineering for local soil conditions
  • California Title 24 solar and energy compliance certs
  • Kern County or City building permit issuance
04 Month 4

Site Excavation & Engineered Foundation

Heavy machinery grades the building pad, digs footings below the local frost line, and installs underground plumbing waste lines and electrical conduits. Steel rebar grids and post-tension cables are placed and inspected before the continuous monolithic concrete pour.

  • Engineered pad compaction and geotechnical signoff
  • Under-slab plumbing and electrical conduit rough-in
  • Kern County pre-pour footing and steel inspection
  • High-strength monolithic post-tension concrete slab pour
05 Month 5

Precision Framing & Weatherproofing

The skeleton of your home rises with kiln-dried Douglas fir lumber, 2x6 exterior walls, and factory-engineered roof trusses designed for Kern County wind and snow loads. High-performance weather barrier and dual-pane low-E argon windows seal the structure.

  • 2x6 exterior wall framing with seismic shear wall nailing
  • Engineered roof truss system & architectural shingles
  • Milgard dual-pane low-E high-mountain glass installation
  • Pre-drywall client framing walkthrough
06 Month 6

Mechanical Rough-Ins & Thermal Envelope

Skilled trade partners install high-efficiency heat pump HVAC ductwork, copper and PEX-A plumbing supply lines, 200A electrical service panels, and low-voltage wiring. High-density R-21 wall and R-38 ceiling insulation creates an airtight thermal envelope.

  • Multi-zone HVAC ductwork & Navien tankless water heater
  • Complete electrical wiring and EV charger rough-in
  • County rough trades inspection signoff
  • Tight-envelope R-21 / R-38 thermal insulation package
07 Month 7–8

Interior Finishes, Cabinetry & Solar

Drywall is hung, taped, and hand-textured. Custom soft-close Shaker cabinetry is set, followed by solid slab quartz countertops, designer tile backsplashes, and luxury flooring. Rooftop solar panels are mounted and tied into the grid.

  • Custom Shaker cabinets with soft-close hinges & drawers
  • Precision solid quartz countertop fabrication & sinks
  • Luxury vinyl plank flooring & designer bathroom tile
  • Rooftop photovoltaic solar package installation and grid tie-in
08 Month 8–9

Final Inspection, Orientation & Warranty

Kern County issues the Certificate of Occupancy. We perform our meticulous 100-point builder punch list and conduct an extensive orientation walk with you. You receive your keys along with your written 1-2-10 warranty and scheduled 11-month return walk.

  • Kern County Certificate of Occupancy final approval
  • Comprehensive buyer orientation and system operation walk
  • Delivery of written 1-2-10 warranty binder and manufacturer manuals
  • Scheduled 11-month return walkthrough date set
