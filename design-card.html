Clear space

X = one quarter of the mark's height. Keep text, photo edges and other logos outside it.

Usage
  • Use the supplied files: aspen2-mark.svg, aspen2-logo.jpg
  • Gold mark on dark photography or Obsidian
  • Bronze mark on Plaster, paper and light walls
  • Don't rotate, skew, outline or add shadows
  • Don't respace the rafters or change their count
  • Don't place the mark on busy mid-tone photos
  • Don't write the name as "Aspen 2" in the logo; the wordmark uses Roman "II"
