Questions

Straight answers.

Still unsure? Call (661) 238-3136 and talk to a person.

How much does it cost to build a home with Aspen II Homes?
Aspen II Homes builds semi-custom homes ranging from roughly $449,000 to $590,000 depending on plan, lot and finish selections. Plans run 1,507 to 2,444 sq ft. Lot cost is separate if you don't already own land, and we'll review feasibility before you commit to anything.
How long does the build take?
Most homes run 7 to 10 months from signed contract to move-in. The two variables that move that number most are Kern County permit review times and weather during foundation and framing. Homes already under construction can close significantly faster.
Can you build on a lot I already own?
Yes. We build on customer-owned lots throughout Kern County. We start with a feasibility review covering setbacks, slope, soils, drainage, utility availability and any HOA architectural requirements, then give you a firm build price before you sign.
What areas do you serve?
We build in Tehachapi, California City, Ridgecrest, Golden Hills, Bear Valley Springs and Stallion Springs — all in Kern County, California. We're familiar with each area's permitting authority, utility districts and, where applicable, HOA architectural review process.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Aspen II Homes, Inc. holds California Contractors State License Board license #1004058 (Class B, General Building Contractor), active through May 31, 2027. We carry an active contractor bond through Hudson Insurance Company and active workers' compensation through State Compensation Insurance Fund. We are BBB rated A+.
What warranty comes with the home?
Every home closes with a written warranty: one year on workmanship and materials, two years on systems, and ten years on structural components. We also schedule an 11-month walkthrough with you so anything on your list gets handled before the one-year coverage expires.
Do you offer anything for military families?
Yes — our Heroes of the Nation program offers dedicated housing options and pricing for active-duty service members and veterans. We regularly build for families stationed at Edwards Air Force Base and Naval Air Weapons Station China Lake.
How much can I actually change in a plan?
Plans are semi-custom, so finishes, fixtures, cabinet layouts, exterior elevation, covered patio and some interior wall configurations can be changed. Structural changes are quoted case by case. You'll see every price before it's locked in.
